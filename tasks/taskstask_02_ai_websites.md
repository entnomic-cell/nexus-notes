\# Task 02 — Make AI Websites Load Correctly



\## Objective

AI-related websites fail to load inside Nexus while normal websites (e.g., Google) work. Diagnose and fix compatibility issues.



\## Success Criteria

\- AI websites load normally.

\- Authentication pages function.

\- No blank screens or blocked scripts.

\- Embedded browsing behaves consistently.



\## Requirements

\- Do not weaken security unnecessarily.

\- Maintain sandbox safety where possible.

\- Fix root cause instead of adding hacks.



\## Investigation Areas

\- WebView or iframe configuration

\- Content Security Policy (CSP)

\- User-agent restrictions

\- Cross-origin permissions

\- JavaScript execution policies

\- Electron BrowserWindow/WebPreferences

\- preload bridge permissions



\## Deliverables

1\. Root cause analysis.

2\. Configuration or code fixes.

3\. Explanation of why AI sites previously failed.

4\. Security impact review.
