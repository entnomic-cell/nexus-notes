\# Task 01 — Restore Context Menu



Problem:

Nexus custom context menu does not appear.



Requirements:

\- Restore Nexus menu.

\- Do not modify unrelated UI systems.



Steps:

1\. Locate contextmenu handler.

2\. Verify renderer/preload permissions.

3\. Restore event binding.



Success Criteria:

Right click shows Nexus context menu globally.
